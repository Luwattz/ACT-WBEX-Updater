Access to these notices
----------------------
Use the application's "许可证" button to read and save all bundled notices.
--export-licenses DIRECTORY exports the same files without opening a window.
ZIP releases also contain THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt, LICENSE.txt and licenses/
next to the executable. Single-file releases embed the texts in the executable;
access does not require Internet connectivity.
